Session review: Fable 5.1 on the HFTC building plans

On 15 October 2026, from 20:00 to 23:51, one Claude Code session running Fable 5.1 turned the 886-page construction bid set for Hayward Fire Station #6 and its Fire Training Center into five things: 117 knowledge docs, a viewer for every sheet, a PDF for a reprographics team, a new campus map, and a PS1-style 3D snow globe of the campus. Fable planned and checked the work and handed most of it to 17 cheaper subagents (9 Haiku, 3 Sonnet, 5 Opus). It paused 75 minutes for the usage window to reset rather than pay for extra usage. Then it took a second ask: put the globe on the web, and redraw the map's floor plans for instructors. Total cost at API prices: $72.57.
